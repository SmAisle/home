Small games, big smiles.

smAisle (say it like "smile"): family-friendly games you can learn in a minute and play solo or with the people who matter to you.

Games

Straight Down

The falling poker puzzle. Cards drop one at a time; line them up in a row or column to make poker hands and clear the board. Solo, endless and pass-and-play modes.

Play in your browseriPhone app coming soon

Connello

A drop-four game with Reversi-style flips. Trap your opponent's discs to turn them your color, and get four of your own in a row to win. Play a friend or the computer.

Play in your browseriPhone app coming soon

Bullseye Roulette

Darts on a spinning roulette wheel. Bet red or black, roll the ball, then hit the number it lands on. Count down from 301 to exactly zero in as few darts as you can. Solo, vs Computer or 2 players.
